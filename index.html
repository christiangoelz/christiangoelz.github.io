About

I build machine learning systems that turn physiological signals and medical images into clinical intelligence.

As a Postdoc at LMU University Hospital Munich, I work across time series analysis for wearable-based seizure prediction and cardiological screening, medical computer vision for coronary angiography, privacy-preserving computing for cross-institutional clinical data, and LLM-powered research tools for biomedical data extraction.

Dr. rer. nat. (summa cum laude); doctoral research in computational neuroscience. Interdisciplinary background in engineering, medicine, and sport science.

Research Focus

Medical Computer Vision

ECG foundation model adaptation, self-supervised representation learning on angiography data, digitalization of the catheter laboratory

Biosignal Analysis & Wearable AI

Seizure detection/prediction from multimodal wearable data (ECG, PPG, EDA, accelerometry), personalized context-aware models

Privacy-Preserving Federated Computing

Secure Multiparty Computation (MPC), Federated Secure Computing platform, cross-border clinical data analytics

Applied LLM & Generative AI

RAG pipelines for biomedical literature, vision-language models for data extraction
